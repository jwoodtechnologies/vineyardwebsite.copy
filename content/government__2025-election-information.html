FINAL ELECTION RESULTS!

On November 18, 2025, the City Council held a special session to certify the results of the November 4, 2025, General Election.
Those elected to office are:
Mayor – Zack Stratton
Councilmembers – David Lauret, Parker McCumber, and Jacob Wood.
In accordance with state code 10-3-201(2), they will take office on January 5, 2026, at 12 o’clock noon.

CASTING OF LOTS
On June 26, 2024, the Vineyard City Council voted to put a change in government to a vote of the people. At an election held on November 5, 2024, Vineyard Residents approved a change in government from a five-member council to a six-member council. As a result of the change in government and in accordance with Utah State Code 10-3b-606, those three (3) Council members were to be elected to office. Also, in accordance with Utah State Code 10-3b-6-606(4) of those three (3) Council members, one is to serve a two-year term, which is to be chosen by lot. This was done at the November 18, 2025, City Council Special Session. Parker McCumber drew the 2-year seat. David Lauret and Jacob Wood will retain the 4-year seat.

With the official ballot drop box at City Hall (125 South Main Street, Vineyard, Utah) now open, we want to remind everyone that City Hall is now considered a voting location under Utah election law. As such, no electioneering is permitted within 150 feet of the drop box. This restriction helps ensure a neutral environment for voters and maintains the integrity of the election process.

For reference, Utah Code §20A-3a-501(1)(a) defines electioneering as:

“Any oral, printed, or written attempt to persuade persons to refrain from voting or to vote for or vote against any candidate or issue.”

This includes, but is not limited to, displaying campaign signs (Including cars, bikes, etc.), distributing literature, wearing campaign apparel (Shirts, hats, buttons, etc.), or engaging in conversations intended to influence a voter’s choice within the restricted area. We appreciate your cooperation in helping us maintain a fair and accessible voting environment for all residents. To help clarify where the restriction applies, below is a photo showing the approximate 150-foot boundary around City Hall. The marked area represents where no campaign activity or materials may be displayed while the ballot drop box is in operation.

Additionally, on November 4, 2025, the City Hall building will be an active polling location, and the 150 feet restriction will apply to the City Hall building as well as the County drop box. Please see the map for the full restricted area.



If you have any questions about the restricted area or how it is measured, please don’t hesitate to contact the Recorder’s Office at 385.338.5183.

General Election Notice

The Utah County Elections Division gives notice of the General Election that will be held on Tuesday, November 4, 2025.

Voters must be citizens of the United States, residents of the voting precinct in which they are voting, and 18 years old or older by the date of the election to participate. All eligible voters will receive their ballot by mail approximately three weeks before Election Day.

A sample ballot and other information relating to the election, including polling places, polling place hours, instructions regarding how an individual with a disability may obtain information on voting in an accessible manner, and qualifications of voters may be obtained from the following sources:
1.    Visiting the Utah County Elections Division’s website: https://vote.utahcounty.gov
2.    Visiting the Statewide Electronic Voter Information Website: https://vote.utah.gov
3.    Visiting the Utah County Elections Division Office
4.    Calling the Utah County Elections Division: (801) 851-VOTE (8683)
5.    Emailing the Utah County Elections Division: elections@utahcounty.gov
6.    Contacting your City Recorder

A public demonstration will be held to test the voting devices and tabulation equipment being used for the noticed election. Anyone who would like to witness the testing is invited to attend, beginning at 10:00 a.m. on October 9, 2025 at the Utah County Ballot Center - 100 E Center Street ROOM LL700 Provo Utah.

All eligible voters will receive their ballot by mail approximately three weeks prior to Election Day.
We strongly encourage voters to return their ballots via the conveniently located drop boxes listed on our website at: vote.utahcounty.gov/voting-locations

2025 VINEYARD CITY MUNICIPAL ELECTION NOTICE

Pursuant to Section 10-3-301(2)(a) of the Utah Code, notice is hereby given that the Municipal Offices in Vineyard, Utah to be voted on in the 2025 Municipal General Election are:

  • Mayor – to be elected at-large to serve a four-year term.
  • City Council Members – three (3) council members will be elected at-large to serve a four-year term with the exception that one member will serve a two-year term. The two-year term will be determined by lots immediately following the election canvass. (UCA 10-3b-606(4))

A Primary Election will be held, if necessary, on Tuesday, August 12, 2025. The General Election will be held on November 4, 2025.

A Vineyard City resident who is interested in becoming a candidate must file a Declaration of Candidacy between June 2 and June 6, 2025. Declarations of Candidacy must be filed, in person, with the Vineyard City Recorder's Office at Vineyard City Hall, 125 South Main Street, Vineyard, Utah, Monday through Friday, 8 am to 5 pm. A $50.00 filing fee is due at the time of filing.

Election information may be obtained at:

  • Vineyard City Recorder’s Office: Pamela Spencer 338.5183, pams@vineyardutah.gov or Tony Lara 385.432.7214, tonyl@vineyardutah.gov
  • Utah County website: https://vote.utahcounty.gov/home
  • Utah State website: https://vote.utah.gov

Filing Qualifications

A person filing a declaration of candidacy for a city office must be (per Utah Code Section 20A-9-201 and 203):

  • A United States citizen at the time of filing;
  • A resident of Vineyard City for the 12 consecutive months immediately preceding the date of the election (or a resident of an area recently annexed into Vineyard City);
  • A registered voter.

In accordance with Utah Constitution Article IV, Section 6, any mentally incompetent person, any person convicted of a felony, or any person convicted of treason or a crime against the elective franchise may not hold office in this state until the right to hold elective office is restored under Utah Code Section 20A-2-101.3 or Section 20A-2-101.5.
