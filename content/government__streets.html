Vineyard City Public Works Department provides top-notch service to our residents, with a focus on improving their quality of life by maintaining clean, safe, and hazard-free roadways. The Streets and Stormwater Division is responsible for overseeing all of the city's public roads. The division provides snow removal, street sweeping, asphalt maintenance, signage, striping, shoulder maintenance, and storm drain maintenance. To ensure efficient and cost-effective snow and ice removal, Vineyard City follows a comprehensive Snow Removal Policy that takes into account the unique variables presented by each weather event. Our staff is dedicated to delivering the highest level of service and responsiveness to citizen requests and concerns, contributing to the overall well-being of Vineyard City residents.
