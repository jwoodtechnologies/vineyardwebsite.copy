ABOUT THE COMMISSION
The Planning Commission consists of 5 regular members and 3 alternate members who are Vineyard residents, appointed by the Mayor. The Planning Commission reviews and approves or disapproves site plans and decides on all applications for Conditional Use Permits. They also make recommendations to City Council on development plans.
