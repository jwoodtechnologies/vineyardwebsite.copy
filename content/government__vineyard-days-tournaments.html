Ski/Snowboard Information 2026-2027

Vineyard Recreation is partnering with Brighton Ski Resort to offer new Ski/Snowboard Lessons to youth ages 8-18. The Ski/Snowboard Program includes a 2-hour lesson, a 3-hour night-ski pass, and transportation to and from Brighton Resort. This program will include three Saturdays this coming winter (12/12, 12/19 & 1/2). A charter bus will leave the Vineyard City Hall Building (125 South Main Street) at exactly 2:30pm and return at approximately 10:30pm. 

Participants are required to bring their own ski or snowboarding equipment, snow clothes, gloves, goggles, etc. When signing up for this program you must choose ski or snowboarding lessons and remain in that class throughout the program. Students are required to check in before boarding the bus at City Hall and at Brighton Resort. Time of drop offs may change depending on driving conditions. 

Each participant will need to fill out Brighton's waiver.
