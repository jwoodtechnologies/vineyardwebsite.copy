Vineyard Recreation's  2027 Pee-Wee Sports Series (4-Year-Old – Pre-K COED) will consist of eight 30-minute sessions to teach basic skills in the following sports: basketball, soccer, T-ball, and flag football. Each clinic consists of up to 16 children (4 children per team). Each team will have a coach provided by Vineyard Recreation Staff. Any parents interested in helping are also welcome.

The Toddler Sports Clinic Series will take place on Tuesday and Thursday afternoons (3:00–3:30pm) OR (3:40-4:10pm) at the Vineyard Elementary indoor gymnasium (620 E Holdaway Rd, Vineyard).

Each child in this session MUST be at least 4 years old by January 5, 2027. No children enrolled/home-schooled in kindergarten as of January 5, 2027, are allowed to participate.
