Vineyard City is working to fill a vacant seat on the Vineyard City Council. The appointed councilmember will serve through December 31, 2027.

Applications Are Now Closed

The application deadline was September 11, 2026, at 12:00 PM. Thank you to everyone who applied and expressed interest in serving the Vineyard community.

Appointment Process

The Vineyard City Council will review the eligible applicants and vote to appoint a new councilmember during a public City Council meeting September 15, 2026 at 6:00pm.
