Vineyard City is a proud partner to One Kind Act a Day and a Certified City of Kindness as of January 2024. Vineyard was the first city to take this pledge, demonstrating a commitment to community and unity. Residents, staff, and businesses have made a commitment to make kindness a cornerstone of daily life in Vineyard.

One Kind Act a Day, a nonprofit dedicated to promoting kindness and community unity, has inspired individual acts of kindness across Utah. Vineyard’s unique approach aims to integrate kindness into every aspect of city life, creating an environment where compassion and empathy thrive.
