Mountainland Association of Governments
"Designated as the Association of Government for our region, MAG strives to accomplish the best work we can for those we serve in Summit, Utah, and Wasatch County, Utah. We thrive on seeing the communities in our area grow and succeed. Our emphasis areas include Informed Aging, Community & Economic Development, and Planning for Growth."

Currently located in Orem and Vineyard, MAG approached Vineyard about locating the entirety of their services under one roof, in the new Vineyard Center. Their organization has outgrown their existing office space and they are eager to be part owners in the Vineyard Center project. As a financial partner, MAG is helping with the cost of design as well as construction for the building. With this partnership, both Vineyard City and MAG are able to cut down on expenses in shared use areas, such as break rooms, fitness centers, conference rooms, large training rooms, and storage rooms.
