Please note: This league is specifically designed for individuals 8–12 years old who have intellectual disabilities. Thank you for registering for Vineyard City's 2025 Street Hockey League! You can expect to hear from your child's coach/Vineyard Administration by Saturday, September 13th.

Age: 8-12
Season: Thursday, September 24th–Thursday October 30th
Registration ($65): July 1st–August 31st *Includes shirt & stick*
Non-Resident Fee: $10
Late Fee: $10

6 Games, Thursday 5:15–6:05
Games will take place at Vineyard's Gammon Park
1 game per week
Each gameday will consist of a 20-minute practice and one 17-minute scrimmage
5v5 with goalies - Participant dependent

50' x 90' Court
50" x 42" Goal
No official score will be kept

Game Schedule
Available on Tuesday, September 9th @ quickscores.com/vineyardutah.

Referees
Vineyard Recreation will provide referees and coaches. Any parent participation is greatly appreciated as you know your child's needs best.

Teams
Each team will have a maximum of 12 players. Teams will be decided each week.

Coaches
We are looking for help from volunteer coaches. If you are interested in being a volunteer coach, please select the option to do so during online registration. Volunteer head coaches may receive up to a 50% credit for future programming for their child's participation in the league (to be granted following the season). Volunteer assistant coaches may receive up to a 25% credit for future programming for their child's participation in the league (to be granted following the season). *Each team is to have 1 head coach and 1 assistant coach.*

In addition to our parent volunteers we will have paid staff to help with coaching and building these kids up.

Age Cutoff
All participants must be aged 8–12 on October 2, 2025.
