In lieu of a bi-annual cleanup, Vineyard will provide dump passes on a yearly basis for residents to use at North Pointe Solid Waste Special Service District. In January, residents will receive a Vineyard dump pass in the mail with two punches available. Residents will be able to use the two punches at any time throughout the year.

How to Get Your Pass
Vineyard dump passes will be sent to residents (renters and owners) who pay sanitation fees through their utility bill. (HOAs that provide their own trash services do not qualify.) To know if you qualify, look for "Sanitation" on your utility bill.
*If you pay sanitation fees for recycling only, you aren't eligible for a pass.

If you don't receive your dump pass in the mail and you qualify for one, come into the city offices to pick up a pass (bring photo ID and proof of address). 

How to Use Your Pass
To use your dump pass, present the pass and photo ID at North Pointe. They'll verify your identity and punch your pass to signify it's been used.

The dump is open:
Monday–Friday: 7:00am–5:30pm
Saturday: 7:30am–3:30pm

North Pointe has limitations on what you can dump. Please review their FAQ page to see what they do and don't accept.

How to Correct Your Pass
If you received a Vineyard dump pass in the mail, but it has outdated information (i.e., the wrong name), bring it into the city offices with photo ID and proof of address, and we can correct our database and provide you with an updated pass.


We're excited to switch to this new system and hope it will provide greater flexibility for discarding waste. If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to us at 801-226-1929.
