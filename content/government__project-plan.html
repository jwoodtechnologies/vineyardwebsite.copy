The Geneva Urban Renewal Area follows the adopted Project Area Plan adopted in 2011. The Project Plan outlines 8 major purposes for redevelopment of the site:

  1. Make possible the redevelopment of over 2,055 acres of under-utilized real estate which has been contaminated and blighted due to over a half century of heavy industrial use. 
  2. Provide the basis for enhanced property and sales tax revenues for the Town, County, School District, other taxing entities and State. 
  3. Create a large number of jobs with a wide range of skill levels. 
  4. Attract national tenants and other job creators looking to build in the Mountain West. 
  5. Make possible alternative transportation hubs to minimize congestion and improve air quality.
  6. Provide a variety of housing types for a wide range of income levels. 
  7. Encourage additional development on the perimeter of the redevelopment area. 
  8. Create a sustainable community.
