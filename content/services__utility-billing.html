Rate Changes Effective January 1, 2026 

Dear Vineyard Residents, 

We want to inform you about an upcoming adjustment to utility rates, which will take effect on January 1, 2026. This change is essential to support the long-term sustainability and efficiency of water and wastewater services. 

Water and Sewer Use Fees 

Vineyard has financial agreements in place to fund critical upgrades to the water and sewer infrastructure. These agreements include modest annual adjustments – 2% for water services and 5% for sewer services – to ensure the city meets inflationary demands while also maintaining reliable operations for our growing community. 

Additionally, the city’s wastewater treatment provider, Timpanogos Special Service District (TSSD), recently increased its treatment rates by 15%. As Vineyard is one of 10 municipalities served by TSSD, this increase is a direct pass-through cost beyond local control. It reflects the rising cost of delivering these essential services. 

Transportation Utility Fee 

Vineyard City is reinstating the Transportation Utility Fee to ensure the continued funding and sustainability of the City’s Transportation Utility Fund. As part of our review process, a rate study was conducted to identify fair, data-driven rates that ensure the Transportation Utility Fund can operate sustainably. As an enterprise fund, this account is required to support itself, meaning the services it provides must be funded directly by the fee. 

The Transportation Utility Fee functions as a utility charge for our road system. Revenue from the Transportation Utility Fee is dedicated to the maintenance, operation, repair, and improvement of local streets and transportation infrastructure. This includes essential services such as asphalt maintenance, sidewalk repairs, traffic signal operations, snow removal, and projects that support pedestrian and bicycle safety. 

Reinstating this fee provides a reliable, long-term funding source to keep Vineyard’s transportation network safe, functional, and well-maintained. 

We understand that rate adjustments can be challenging, and we remain committed to transparency and ensuring that every dollar supports the safe and efficient delivery of vital utilities to Vineyard residents. Thank you for your understanding and support.

New Residents
You are automatically signed up for recycling. If you wish to opt out please fill out the form (PDF). You have 30 days from your move-in date to complete this form.

The annual opt-out period for recycling will be done in the month of September but you can sign up for recycling at any time throughout the year. The opt-out form must be turned in by September 30.

After completing the form please email it to the Admin department (info@vineyardutah.org).
