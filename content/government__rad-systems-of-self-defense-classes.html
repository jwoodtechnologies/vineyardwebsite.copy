R.A.D. Women classes are coming to Vineyard! These free courses are offered in affiliation and partnership with R.A.D. Systems of Self-Defense and the Utah County Sheriff’s Office.

About the Course

The Rape Aggression Defense (R.A.D.) System is a comprehensive, women-only course that teaches realistic self-defense tactics and techniques. The course begins with awareness, prevention, risk reduction, and risk avoidance before progressing to hands-on defense training.

Participants will learn how to visually identify vulnerable areas, physically respond to an attack, and create opportunities to escape a violent encounter.

Course Details

  • Who: Females ages 12 and older

  • Dates: March 1, 8, 15, and 22, 2027

  • Time: 6:00–9:00 p.m. each Monday

  • Length: Four classes totaling 12 hours

  • Location: Vineyard, Utah

  • Cost: Free for Utah County residents

The exact class address will be provided to registered participants by email before the course begins.

Physical or Medical Accommodations

Participants with medical or physical conditions requiring an accommodation should contact the Utah County Sheriff’s Office before the course. Staff will discuss available support and work to provide a safe, empowering educational experience whenever possible.

Women at any stage of pregnancy may attend and observe the course but may not participate in its physical portions.
