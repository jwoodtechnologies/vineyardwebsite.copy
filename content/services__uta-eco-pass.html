How do I get a pass?
Visit Vineyard City Hall with proof of residency (such as a utility bill, lease agreement, or ID) to receive your physical Eco-Pass today!

How much does it cost?
The Eco-Pass costs $59 per month. Payments can be made at Vineyard City Hall or online via Podium (a 3% fee applies to credit/debit card payments). If payment is not received by the 3rd business day of the month, the pass will be deactivated until payment is made.

What does the Eco-Pass include?
The Eco-Pass provides unlimited travel for one calendar month on local buses, express buses, TRAX light rail, Streetcar light rail, bus rapid transit, and FrontRunner commuter rail.
