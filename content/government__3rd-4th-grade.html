Game Schedule
Available on Tuesday, December 8, 2026 @ https://www.quickscores.com/vineyardutah

Practices
- Each team will be assigned up to 4 practices during the season @ Vineyard Elementary or Franklin Discovery Academy.
- Each practice will last up to 45 minutes.
- Potential practice times are Monday-Friday @ 4:00pm–8:00pm.
- Practice time selection is left to the team coach(es).

Referees
Each game will consist of 1–2 referee(s) provided by Vineyard Recreation Staff.

Teams
Each team will have a maximum of 8 players.

Youth Sports Team Formation Process:
For those who register before the deadline (and before the league is full):

  1. Head-to-head request (guaranteed)
  2. Coach’s roster requests
  3. Neighborhood
  4. Age/grade/gender balancing

Coaches
We are looking for volunteer coaches. If you are interested in being a volunteer coach, please select the option during online registration.
*Each team should have 1 Head Coach and 1 Assistant Coach*

Age Cutoff
All participants must be in correct grade as of the 2026–2027 school year.
No players are allowed to play down a division.
Players may play up one age level (upon administrator's approval) IF the child's parent/guardian is willing to help coach.
