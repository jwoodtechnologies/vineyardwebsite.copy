The city streets division is tasked with the critical responsibility of maintaining the road infrastructure within the municipality. Their duties encompass a wide range of activities to preserve road safety and functionality. This includes promptly repairing potholes, resurfacing roads to prevent deterioration, sealing cracks to minimize water damage, and repainting markings for visibility. Additionally, they remove snow and ice during winter, ensuring safe passage for vehicles and pedestrians. Routine tasks such as sign maintenance, street sweeping, and conducting inspections to identify maintenance needs are also part of their responsibilities. Through these efforts, the city streets division is vital in facilitating efficient transportation and enhancing the overall quality of life for residents and commuters alike.
