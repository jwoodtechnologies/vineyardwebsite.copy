Protecting Our Community for the Future

Stormwater management doesn't end when construction is complete. Long-term stormwater management helps ensure that drainage systems, ponds, swales, and other stormwater facilities continue to function properly for years to come.

Routine maintenance and inspections help protect water quality, reduce flooding, and preserve the health of our community and Utah Lake.


Stormwater facilities are designed to:

  • Reduce flooding
  • Improve water quality
  • Prevent erosion
  • Capture sediment and pollutants
  • Protect local waterways and Utah Lake

Regular maintenance helps these systems continue working as intended.


Maintaining Stormwater Facilities

Stormwater systems require ongoing care to remain effective. Maintenance activities may include:

  • Inspecting stormwater facilities
  • Removing sediment and debris
  • Maintaining drainage channels and swales
  • Cleaning storm drains and catch basins
  • Repairing damaged infrastructure
  • Managing vegetation around stormwater facilities


Everyone Plays a Role

Property owners, businesses, developers, and the City all play an important role in protecting stormwater infrastructure.

You can help by:

  • Keeping storm drains free of leaves and debris.
  • Properly disposing of trash and household chemicals.
  • Never dumping anything into storm drains.
  • Reporting damaged or clogged stormwater facilities.


Report a Stormwater Concern

If you notice a damaged storm drain, blocked drainage channel, or other stormwater issue, please let us know.

Examples include:

  • Clogged storm drains
  • Damaged catch basins
  • Flooding or drainage issues
  • Excessive sediment buildup
  • Damaged stormwater facilities
