The City Engineer is a department that issues development construction permits and deals with safety concerns regarding traffic control. Builders, contractors, or anyone performing work in Vineyard needs to visit our office to obtain a permit for any work that involves:
  • Stormwater Pollution Prevention Plan (SWPPP)
  • Land Disturbance Permit Site Development
  • Utilities Connection
  • Residential Grading Sidewalk
  • Curb or Gutter Installations
  • Right-of-Way Closure
This also applies to any residents performing any work that ties into public right-of-way such as changes to driveways, utility connections, and extensive grading of yards.
