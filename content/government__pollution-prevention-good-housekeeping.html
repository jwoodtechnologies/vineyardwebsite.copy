What Is Pollution Prevention?

Pollution prevention means stopping pollutants before they reach the storm drain system. Since stormwater flows directly to local waterways without treatment, preventing pollution is one of the most effective ways to protect water quality.




Our Public Works Department helps prevent stormwater pollution by:

  • Sweeping streets to remove dirt, leaves, and debris
  • Cleaning and maintaining storm drains and catch basins
  • Inspecting stormwater facilities
  • Maintaining parks, trails, and public spaces
  • Properly storing and handling maintenance materials
  • Training employees on stormwater best management practices (BMPs)
  • Responding to spills and stormwater concerns

How You Can Help

Everyone plays a role in protecting our waterways. You can help by:

  • Never dumping anything into storm drains
  • Picking up pet waste
  • Properly disposing of motor oil, paint, and household chemicals
  • Keeping leaves, grass clippings, and trash out of gutters
  • Washing vehicles at a commercial car wash or on grassy areas when appropriate
  • Reporting spills or illegal dumping

Every small action helps keep Vineyard's waterways clean.
