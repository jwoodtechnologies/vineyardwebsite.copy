Short-Term Rentals Are Not Permitted in Vineyard

Vineyard City does not permit short-term rentals (such as Airbnb, VRBO, or similar vacation rental platforms) in residential zoning districts.

Properties advertised or operated as short-term rentals are considered to be in violation of the Vineyard Zoning Code and may be subject to enforcement actions and civil penalties.

Applicable City Code

VZC 15.12.050 – District Use Table
VZC 15.12.040 – Vineyard Zoning Map

Enforcement

If Vineyard City determines that a property is being used as an illegal short-term rental, the property owner may receive:

- A notice of violation
- Civil penalties
- Additional daily penalties

Listings advertised on platforms such as VRBO, Airbnb, or similar websites may be used as evidence during the enforcement process.
