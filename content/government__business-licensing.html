Thank you for your interest in locating your business in Vineyard, Utah! The rapidly-growing community of Vineyard is an excellent place to locate your business.

WHO MUST OBTAIN A BUSINESS LICENSE
If you engage in any business out of a physical location within the boundaries of Vineyard, you are required to maintain a valid, unexpired business license.  This includes home-based businesses.  Please note that registering a business name with the State of Utah Department of Commerce does NOT license a business with Vineyard.

HOW TO APPLY
Please click on the appropriate link for your business license type to access the online application, and reach out to Business License Specialist Kelly Kloepfer with any questions.
