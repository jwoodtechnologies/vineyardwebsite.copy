Protecting Water Quality During Construction

Construction activities can increase the amount of sediment, debris, and pollutants that enter the storm drain system. Vineyard City works with contractors and developers to ensure best management practices (BMPs) are used to protect local waterways throughout construction projects.

Proper stormwater management helps prevent erosion, reduces pollution, and protects our community and Utah Lake.

Stormwater Best Management Practices (BMPs)

Construction sites should implement practices such as:

  • Installing erosion and sediment controls
  • Protecting storm drains from sediment and debris
  • Stabilizing disturbed soil
  • Properly storing construction materials
  • Managing concrete washout areas
  • Preventing pollutants from leaving the construction site

Report a Construction Stormwater Concern

If you observe sediment, muddy runoff, or other pollutants leaving a construction site, please let us know.

Examples include:

  • Mud or sediment washing into streets or storm drains
  • Poor erosion control measures
  • Construction debris entering waterways
  • Concrete washout in unauthorized areas
  • Other stormwater pollution concerns
