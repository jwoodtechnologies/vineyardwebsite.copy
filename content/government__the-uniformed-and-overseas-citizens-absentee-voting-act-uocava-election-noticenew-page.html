The Uniformed and Overseas Citizens Absentee Voting Act (UOCAVA) Election Notice
UCA 20A-16-502 May 1, 2025

Dear Voter,

This notice pertains to voters covered under the Uniformed and Overseas Absentee Voting Act (UOCAVA) that are eligible to vote in the upcoming election.

This is a municipal election year and Vineyard, Utah will have one mayoral seat and three council seats up for election (four-year terms with one exception see our initial election notice for details.) Utah County will be gaining a new city: Spring Lake, Utah. There will be an election for their mayor and city council seats. Alpine School District (ASD) will also be splitting into 3 school boards. If you are part of the ASD, you will also have your new school board seat on your ballot.

For information on the ASD split or the new city election, reach out to the Elections Office.

IMPORTANT ELECTION DATES

UOCAVA ballots are sent: 06/27/2025
Last day to request a ballot: 8/11/2025
Postmark deadline: 8/11/2025
Primary Election Day: 8/12/2025
Election canvass: 8/26/2025

Electronic ballots must be received by the Elections Oﬃce by 8:00 PM (Mountain Standard Time) on Election Day, Tuesday, August 12, 2025. Mailed ballots must be postmarked no later than the day before Election Day, Monday, August 11, 2025, and received up through the Election Canvass. For more information on the use of the Federal Post Card Application (FPCA), the Federal Voting Assistance Program (fvap.gov), and related deadlines, or the use of a Federal Write-In Absentee Ballot (FWAB), please visit Utah County Election Division website at vote.utahcounty.gov. If you do not receive a ballot on time, please contact the Elections Office directly at 801-851-8128, or Elections@UtahCounty.gov.
