Who needs to obtain a Special Event Permit?

If you are organizing an event for a group of fifty or more people within the boundaries of Vineyard then you will need a Special Events Permit. If you are planning a block party, you will not need a Special Event Permit. If you are planning a Block Party that will require a road closure,
please submit a Block Party Courtesy Notice.

Complete applications must be submitted to the city no later than thirty (30) days prior to the event.
