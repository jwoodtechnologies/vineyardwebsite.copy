What Is Stormwater?

Stormwater is rainwater or snowmelt that flows over rooftops, streets, parking lots, and other surfaces instead of soaking into the ground. As it travels, it can pick up pollutants such as oil, fertilizer, pet waste, litter, and chemicals. Unlike wastewater, stormwater is not treated before it enters storm drains, which flow directly to local waterways. By working together to keep stormwater clean, we help protect our community, wildlife, and Utah Lake for future generations.

Did you know? Stormwater isn't treated before it reaches local waterways.
