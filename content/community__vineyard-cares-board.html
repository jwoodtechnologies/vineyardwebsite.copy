Vineyard Cares is a community coalition run by a community board and city staff member. Our mission is to prevent and reduce substance misuse as well as spread awareness regarding mental health issues among youth and families living in Vineyard, UT. Vineyard Cares is working to create an inclusive and thriving community where mental, social, emotional, and physical wellness are prioritized, empowering residents to lead fulfilling and healthy lives

BOARD VS. COALITION
The Vineyard Cares Board is responsible for making decisions and maintaining the coalition as a whole. They act as a voice for the coalition, determining where the coalition focuses attention and funds. The board reports to key leaders such as the Mayor and funders and oversee the bylaws of the coalition.

The Vineyard Cares Coalition is made up of local residents and businesses who want to participate in planning events and programs related to substance abuse awareness. They meet monthly to coordinate responsibilities and plan activities or classes for the community.
