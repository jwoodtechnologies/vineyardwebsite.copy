SPLASH PAD RULES

  • This facility is unsupervised – play at your own risk
  • Adult supervision is recommended
  • Do not drink the water
  • Running, boisterous, or rough play is prohibited
  • No climbing or hanging on equipment
  • No animals allowed in the splash pad at any time
  • No glass containers, skateboarding, bicycles or scooters allowed
  • Children not toilet trained must wear swimming diapers and tightly fitting plastic pants
  • Splash pad may close at any time due to weather or maintenance concerns
  • In an emergency, dial 911
  • For problems with the splash pad, dial 385.215.4060
  • Be courteous. Take turns.
  • Only one child allowed on the slide at a time.
