There are two ways to pay your bill electronically. Vineyard offers a Direct Pay option where the bill will automatically be taken from your bank account on or around the 20th of each month. You can view and print the Direct Pay Authorization (PDF) online. You can email the completed form to the department. This option is only available for homeowners.

You may also make your utility payments online by setting up your account with XPress Billpay! This is an easy option for renters and those wanting to pay with a credit card. An auto pay option is available through XPress Bill Pay.

Important Note: If you were signed up for Direct Pay and need to update banking info, please call Vineyard City at 801-226-1929. Direct Pay and XPress Bill Pay are two separate entities that do not communicate with each other.
