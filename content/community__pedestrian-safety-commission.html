Pedestrian Safety Commission

Help make Vineyard safer for everyone! Applications are now open for five regular members and two alternates to serve on Vineyard’s Pedestrian Safety Commission.

Formerly the Active Transportation Commission, the commission has been renamed and reorganized with a renewed focus on the safety of people walking, biking, using mobility devices, and traveling through Vineyard’s streets, crossings, sidewalks, and trails. All seats are open, and former members are welcome to apply.

What the Commission Does

The commission advises the Mayor and City Council on pedestrian safety by:

  • Identifying safety concerns at crossings, intersections, school routes, and other public spaces.

  • Reviewing traffic and safety information.

  • Recommending safety improvements and traffic calming measures.

  • Supporting public education and coordination with City staff, schools, and public safety partners.

Time Commitment

The commission will hold one regular meeting each month and may meet more frequently as needed. Members should also be prepared to review meeting materials and participate in commission responsibilities.

Appointments are anticipated to be completed by the end of November 2027, with the first meeting planned for early December.
