Working through a competitive bid process, Method Studios was selected from a pool of 16 architectural firms to design the Vineyard Center. Method has demonstrated their talent in designing familiar buildings such as: Orem City Hall, The Ruth, The Noorda Center for the Performing Arts, and many other projects throughout the state. You can view their projects here.

Design work for the Vineyard Center began in 2022 with a spatial analysis from Nelson Partners to determine how much space was needed to accommodate Vineyard City and MAG. It was determined that a 4 story building located on Block 10A of the Utah City Development would be sufficient for this project. From that spatial analysis, Method Studio has been working on refining it and designing a building that will meet staff and community needs. 

One unique aspect of this project is the building location. Flagborough donated 1.25 acres to Vineyard City right off of the Promenade Park set to open in Summer of 2025. The ground floor of the Vineyard Center is being designed in a way to essentially expand the park onto the ground floor of the building. There will be space for public markets, community meeting rooms, and other community services.
