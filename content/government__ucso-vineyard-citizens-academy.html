USCO Vineyard Citizens Academy will be held Wednesdays starting February 24, March 3, 10, 17, 24 & 31 2027

The UCSO Vineyard Citizens Academy is an opportunity for community members to learn more about the Utah County Sheriff’s Office, meet the people behind the badge, and better understand the services provided to our community.

Participants will get an inside look at law enforcement operations, including patrol procedures, investigations, communications, emergency response, crime prevention, and other areas of public safety. The academy encourages open communication, builds positive relationships, and provides insight into the challenges and responsibilities of law enforcement.

Participants must be 18 or older. By registering, participants commit to attending the academy in its entirety.

Join us for an engaging, hands-on experience and discover what it takes to serve and protect our community! Space may be limited, so please complete the registration form to reserve your spot.

Not to be confused with the one time event, Nov. 4th Vineyard Mini Academy: The citywide Vineyard Mini Academy is a separate event where you can learn about City departments and services. No registration is required for the Vineyard Mini Academy.
