Vineyard City is committed to protecting your privacy. When the Finance Department requests or collects personal data, this notice is provided in compliance with Utah Code 63A-19-402 to inform you about how your information will be used. 

1. Why the Finance Department Is Requesting Your Personal Data 

The Finance Department collects personal data to deliver Vineyard City services, process applications and reimbursements, invoice for services, rentals, and sponsorships, and coordinate with partner agencies and departments. Providing this information helps the Finance Department efficiently serve you and fulfill governmental responsibilities. 

2. Intended Purposes and Uses of Your Personal Data 

Your personal data will be used for the following purposes:  

  • Process Utility and Hydrant Meter Applications 
  • Process Utility Payments 
  • Manage Eco-Pass enrollment with Utah Transit Authority (UTA) 
  • Administer Recreation and Library reimbursements 
  • Provide address and contact details to support Neighborhood Services and the Sheriff’s Department 
  • Invoice for special services, rentals, and sponsorships 
  • Provide regular notifications and announcements from Vineyard City
3. Consequences of Refusing to Provide Personal Data 


Providing personal data is mandatory for this service. If you choose not to provide the requested information:  

  • Your application will not be processed, and you will be ineligible for city utilities   
4. Data Sharing 


Your personal data may be shared with the following:  

  • Entities that share personal data with the Finance Department: Vineyard’s Building & Planning Departments, the Sheriff’s Department, Public Works Department, Communication Department. 
  • Entities that receive personal data from the Finance Department on a regular or contractual basis: Vineyard Neighborhood Service Coordinator, Communication Department, Sheriff’s Department, Utah Transit Authority (UTA), Zions Bank, CC Bank.
5. Record Series


Personal data collected by the Finance Department will be included in the following record series: 

  • RS2206 
  • RS507
6. Your Rights and Additional Information 


You have the right to request a copy of this notice regarding personal data you previously provided. To request this information or to ask questions about how your data is handled, please contact:  
Vineyard City Privacy Manager  
privacy@vineyardutah.gov  

This notice is available:  

  • At the point of data collection  
  • Upon request  

By providing your personal data, you acknowledge that you have been informed of how your information will be used and shared in accordance with this notice.
