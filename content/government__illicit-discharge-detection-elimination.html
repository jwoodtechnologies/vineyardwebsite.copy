Help us protect Vineyard's waterways by reporting suspected illicit discharges or stormwater pollution. If you notice anything entering a storm drain that shouldn't be there, please let us know.

Click Here to Report a Concern

Examples of concerns include:

  • Illegal dumping
  • Oil or chemical spills
  • Paint or concrete washout
  • Sewage or wastewater discharges
  • Soapy water entering a storm drain
  • Unusual odors, discoloration, or foam in waterways

If you witness an illicit discharge or have concerns about stormwater pollution, please contact Vineyard City's Public Works Department.
