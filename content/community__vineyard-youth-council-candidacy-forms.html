Applications are now available for students interested in running for a Vineyard Youth Council Executive Board position. Candidates can submit a Declaration of Candidacy form for any of the following positions:

  • Youth Mayor
  • Youth City Manager
  • Youth Recorder
  • Communications Chair
  • Activities Chair
  • Service & Beautification Chair

Complete the appropriate fillable form below to declare your candidacy. Executive Board elections will be held Monday, September 7.
Please email completed forms to tonyl@vineyardutah.gov.
