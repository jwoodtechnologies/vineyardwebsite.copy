Use the above links to apply for a city parking permit. Currently, the city has a parking permit program for the following areas:
  • Providence Neighborhood
  • 100 West and 470 North in the Springs Neighborhood
Permit Rules and Regulations
  • Permits are only valid for parking on designated public streets
  • Street parking is prohibited during snowfall
  • Vehicles parked illegally may be towed or booted at the owner's expense
  • Permits may be revoked as a penalty for violations
  • Parking is available on a first-come, first-served basis. Once all designated parking spaces are occupied, no additional vehicles can be accommodated


Parking Permit FAQ
  • How many permits am I able to purchase? 
    • Providence and Springs neighborhoods are able to purchase up to 3 permits per household. 
  • If I am a landlord, should I register and account or should my tenants?
    • We encourage whoever is living at the property to sign up for the permit. As part of the sign-up process, the account holder must personally review and agree to the rules and regulations set by Vineyard City. Additionally, they may need to update vehicle information or renew their permit in the future, and creating an account for them could lead to complications if they are unfamiliar with how to manage these actions.
  • How many vehicles can be listed on 1 permit?
    • You can list all of your vehicles on 1 permit, however, only 1 vehicle per permit is able to be activated for street parking. Be sure that the vehicle that is parked is the vehicle and license plate that you have selected on your account.
  • How long is my permit valid for?
    • Permits are valid for 1 year from date of activation. When your permit is about to expire, you will receive email notifications from our vendor letting you know to renew.
  • Do I have to purchase an entire year pass?
    • Yes, all permits are sold on a 1-year basis. If you are moving and no longer need the pass, you can let the city know and we can void the pass going forward.
