Vineyard 101 Mini Academy

Meet the people. Ask questions. Get to know your City.

Discover how Vineyard works and connect with the people who help keep our community running! Join us for an evening of conversation with City staff, where you can visit department tables, ask questions, and learn about the services and projects shaping Vineyard.

Wednesday, November 4, 2026
5:30–7:30 PM

707 E Mill Road, Suite #201
Vineyard, UT 84059

A New Format for a Familiar Program

Vineyard 101 Mini Academy replaces the City’s previous Vineyard Citizens Academy with a condensed, one-day experience. This new roundtable and open-house format brings City departments together in one place, making it easier for residents to meet staff and learn how each department serves the community.

What to Expect

  • Meet City staff: Get to know the people behind Vineyard’s services.

  • Visit department tables: Chat directly with representatives from each department about their work.

  • Ask questions: Bring your questions about City services, processes, and projects.

  • Explore what’s happening: Learn about current efforts and opportunities to get involved.

No registration is required. Stop by anytime between 5:30 and 7:30 PM!

Vineyard 101 Mini Academy is a Citywide event and is separate from the UCSO Vineyard Citizens Academy, which focuses on Sheriff’s Office operations and requires registration.
