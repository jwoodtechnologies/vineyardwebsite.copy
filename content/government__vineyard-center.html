Vineyard Center is a newly proposed multi-use building that will house Vineyard City Offices, the Mountainland Association of Governments (MAG), an additional future tenant as well as community space and amenities. In February 2025, Vineyard City and MAG selected Method Studios as the project's architects.

Located on Block 10A of the Utah City development, the site offers direct access to the promenade, creating a dynamic ground-floor community space. Surface parking will be available on a neighboring block, with plans for a parking structure to the north.
