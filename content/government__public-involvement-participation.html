Thank you for your comment and helping keep Vineyard City’s stormwater system clean and our environment healthy!

Protecting our waterways is a community effort. Vineyard City encourages residents, businesses, and visitors to take an active role in preventing stormwater pollution. By working together, we can help keep our neighborhoods, parks, and Utah Lake clean for future generations.



There are many simple ways to make a positive impact:

  • Pick up litter in your neighborhood or local park.
  • Properly dispose of pet waste.
  • Keep leaves, grass clippings, and debris out of storm drains.
  • Report illegal dumping or stormwater pollution.
  • Volunteer during community cleanup events.
  • Share stormwater education with your family and neighbors.

Community Cleanups

Vineyard City periodically hosts community cleanup opportunities that help keep our streets, parks, trails, and waterways clean. These events are a great way to get involved while making a positive impact in our community.

Check our Events Calendar for upcoming cleanup opportunities!

Report a Stormwater Concern

If you see anything that could impact stormwater quality, please let us know.

Examples include:

  • Illegal dumping
  • Oil or chemical spills
  • Clogged storm drains
  • Flooding concerns
  • Construction site runoff
