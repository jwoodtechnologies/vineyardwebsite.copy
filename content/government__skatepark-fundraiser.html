All-Wheels Concrete Skate Park: Following the request for proposal process, Vineyard City chose to partner with Hunger Skateparks to create an All-Wheels Concrete Skate Park design. We would like to thank the Vineyard Community for the feedback you provided regarding the features you would like to see in the park. Public engagement efforts included an in-person Open House, two surveys (with a total of 249 submitted responses), and various social media posts (with a total of approximately 75 comments).

In February of 2026, the Vineyard City All-Wheels Concrete Skate Park final design was completed. The 16,000 square foot facility is to be located on City-owned property just southeast of the Vineyard City Hall Building (125 S. Main St, Vineyard, UT 84059). Durable cast-in-place concrete construction, which is considered the industry standard for municipal skate parks, will help ensure long-term performance, reduce maintenance needs, and improve safety in various ways for users.

The overall scope of this project shall consist of two phases. Phase one includes an All-Wheels Concrete Skate Park, 24 added parking stalls (one of which being ADA-compliant), an ADA-compliant ramp park entrance, approximately 375’ of added Asphalt Trail to connect two existing trail networks, landscaping, a restroom facility, utility line extension for potential outdoor lighting, two benches, two signage posts, and three single-inverted-U bike racks. Phase two may include an asphalt pump track, pavilion shade structure adjacent to the All-Wheels Concrete Skate Park, and outdoor lighting surrounding the pump track and skate park.

Due to the Vineyard City All-Wheels Concrete Skate Park being designed to have terrain suitable for both beginner and advanced users, this recreational amenity is intended to serve users of varying ages and skill levels. The park will be welcoming of skateboarders, scooter-riders, roller bladers, BMX bikers, and adaptive riders.

The total estimated project cost for completion of Phase 1 is $1,420,000. In March of 2026, Vineyard City submitted a Utah Outdoor Recreation Grant application to request for a 50/50 match amount of $694,500. Pending the receipt of this grant award in May of 2026, Vineyard City plans to begin construction in the Summer of 2026, with the Phase 1 grand opening to occur in June of 2027!

To fund Vineyard City’s required portion of the 50/50 grant match, the Vineyard Mayor and City Council approved via resolution on March 10th, 2026 to allocate current and future Vineyard RAP Tax funds to build the facility.

Not only will this facility expand outdoor recreation within Vineyard City for teenagers and young adults, but it will also provide an individual-based recreation opportunity to the public and drive economic impact for the Community. The location of the facility will allow for convenient oversight with public safety offices nearby to help promote responsible use and stewardship of the facility.
