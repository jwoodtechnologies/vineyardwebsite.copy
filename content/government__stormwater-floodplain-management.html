Stormwater & Floodplain Management

Stormwater is rainwater or snowmelt that flows over streets, sidewalks, parking lots, and other surfaces instead of soaking into the ground. As it travels, it can carry pollutants such as oil, trash, fertilizers, and pet waste into local waterways.

Floodplain management focuses on reducing flood risks by protecting areas that are prone to flooding and promoting responsible development. Together, stormwater and floodplain management help protect our community, improve water quality, reduce flooding, and preserve our natural resources for future generations.

Explore the sections above to learn how Vineyard City manages stormwater, protects waterways, and how you can help keep our community clean and safe!

Report a Spill or Illicit Discharge
Vineyard City is committed to protecting our waterways and complying with Utah’s UPDES Permit UTR090000. If you witness or suspect any unauthorized dumping, spills, or other illicit discharges into our storm drains, creeks, or open ditches, please let us know immediately. Your report helps us keep our community and environment safe!
